# Sprint 2 — Motion Characterization

## Goal

Compare KLT motion signatures across controlled behaviours.

## Pilot observations

Whole-face KLT showed a clear high-motion signature for head nodding. Blinking produced shorter motion spikes, while the simulated drowsy sequence produced relatively low whole-face motion.

The observations indicated that raw motion magnitude alone was not sufficient for drowsiness interpretation.

## Key lesson

Temporal ROI-specific motion and tracking reliability should be evaluated together rather than relying on a single whole-face speed value.
